Privacy Policy — Tokyo's Chess Academy

Effective date: October 17, 2026
Operator: Tokyo's Chess Academy, run by Steven Moultrie
Contact: stevenmoultrie.jobs@gmail.com

Tokyo's Chess Academy ("the Academy", "we", "us") runs live chess classes for kids and adults through the Tokyo's Chess Academy app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

What we collect

When a student (or a parent/guardian signing up a child) creates a profile and books a class, we collect:

  • Profile details: name, email address, phone number, age, skill level, and an optional profile photo.
  • Bookings: the class, date, and session booked, plus payment and class-history records.
  • Class activity: attendance, chess ratings and match records from in-app games, challenge results, and credit balances.
  • Messages: messages a student sends to the instructor through the app's Message Center, and preset reactions ("Good game!", "Well played!", etc.) sent between students.
  • Reviews: star ratings and written reviews shared after class.
  • Contract: the signed class contract (name, signature, date) that a parent/guardian signs before a child joins.

Live video and audio

Classes happen over live video and audio. The live stream is relayed in real time so the class can happen. We do not record classes. We do not store video or audio from classes.

Children's privacy

Our classes serve children. Children under 13 must be signed up by a parent or guardian, who signs the class contract before the child joins — that signed contract is how we get a parent's permission to collect the child's profile and booking information. We collect only what is needed to run the classes, we do not use children's information for advertising, and we never sell any information.

A parent or guardian can ask us to review, correct, or delete their child's information at any time by contacting us at the email above. We will respond promptly.

What is shown publicly

Five-star reviews (reviewer's name, star rating, class, date, and review text) are shown publicly in the app. Nothing else in a student's profile is public. Profiles, messages, bookings, credits, and match records are visible only to the student and the instructor.

How we use information

  • To run classes, take bookings, and track each student's progress.
  • To send booking confirmations and class messages.
  • To operate challenges, ratings, credits, and parent progress reports.
  • To keep students safe: preset reactions only (no free-text chat between students), plus block and report tools.

Who else handles information

We share information only as needed to run the app, with:

  • LiveKit — relays live video and audio during classes.
  • Stripe — processes card payments through payment links. Stripe handles card details; we never see or store card numbers.
  • Vercel — hosts the small server that issues class join codes and stores class data.
  • Apple — distributes the app through the App Store and TestFlight.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party advertising in the app.

Payments

Payments are made through Stripe payment links or Cash App. Payment providers handle the payment itself under their own privacy policies. We keep a record of the amount paid and the credits it buys (1 credit = 1 class), which is how class payments are tracked.

Security and retention

Class data is stored on our server and on the instructor's device. Instructor-only areas of the app require a passphrase. We keep a student's information while they are enrolled and for a reasonable time afterward for class records, and we delete it when a parent or student asks us to (see below).

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your (or your child's) information, or to stop using it, by emailing stevenmoultrie.jobs@gmail.com. If you delete your profile, your bookings and class records are removed with it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version at this same address and update the effective date above.
